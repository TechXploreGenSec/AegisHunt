Graph-computed asset progress — single-asset depth enforcement. Four tools:

asset_set_current(programId, assetKey, priority?) — declare the ONE asset under test. Do not call for a new asset until the current one is complete or fully blocked.

asset_progress_get(programId, assetKey) — compute real testing percent from the graph (endpoints → hypotheses → test executions + explicit blockers). Call after every test_execution_record, not just at the end. Never state a percentage yourself; call this and report what it returns.

asset_mark_blocker(endpointKey, reason, attemptedWorkaround) — record an endpoint that cannot be tested, with what was tried. Blocked counts as terminal progress, not skipping.

asset_get_queue(programId) — list incomplete assets in run order. Pick the next asset from here only after the current one reports isComplete.
